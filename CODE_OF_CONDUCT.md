# Code of conduct

Shoji is a small project and we want it to stay a calm place to work on it.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep feedback specific and useful. "This breaks X because Y" beats "this is bad".
- Respect that people have different experience, time and backgrounds.
- Keep discussion on topic: the browser, its code and its users.

## What we will not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, or using the project for promotion unrelated to it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules,
and may restrict participation for repeat or serious violations.

To report a problem, use GitHub's *Report content* on the comment, or open a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/KJX469/shoji/security/advisories/new) addressed to the
maintainers if it can't be raised publicly.
